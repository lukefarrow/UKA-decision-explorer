How to use this explorer

Interpret the outputs as treatment trade-offs, not a recommendation score.

The app assumes the patient is already a reasonable candidate for medial UKA. It compares outcome domains where current evidence can inform counselling: PROMs, joint awareness, revision, early safety, same-day discharge, recovery, activity and movement.
